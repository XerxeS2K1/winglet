# Winglet v0.12.2

A small release: smoother streaming in several chats, a more honest bots view, and a cut-off reply no longer looks like it is still typing.

## Smoother while several chats stream

- Streamed updates are now grouped per frame, and rows that did not change are kept as they were, so several chats streaming at once should stay smoother.
- The bot picker (the chip and the open list) no longer redraws while a reply streams.
- **Cache saving is back to about once a second.** 0.12.1 saved a streaming chat to the phone's cache at most every 5 seconds. That is reverted, because the app felt slower. If the app is killed in the middle of a reply, the cached copy can now miss at most about the last second. A finished reply is saved within a second, and leaving the app saves everything straight away. The copy on the server is not affected.
- **Not proven on a phone.** The speed work was measured with emulated benchmarks on the server. How much you notice on your phone is not proven yet.

## The bots view shows what a bot is really doing

- A bot shows as "Working" when Hermes has a started hand-off for it, even when the gateway's live-work check does not answer. When the gateway does answer, its answer wins. That hand-off, and any queued ones, appear in the queue sheet.
- Optional: if you run a job dispatcher that keeps one `job-*.json` record per job in a folder (named by `BOT_DISPATCH_HOME`, else `<hermes home>/jobs`), the queue sheet also lists its running and pending stages. This is read-only, and without such a folder nothing changes. The exact rules are in docs/BOTS.md in the repository.

## A reply cut off by the app being killed

If the app was killed while a reply was still streaming, that reply used to come back from the phone's cache with a cursor that never ended. It now comes back as finished, with a small "May be incomplete" caption. The server's copy replaces it the next time the chat syncs. This closes issue #48.

## Upgrading

Update the app from the in-app update, and the plugin from the release zip or `hermes plugins update`. Going from 0.12.1 to 0.12.2 has not been tried through the plugin's self-update yet.
